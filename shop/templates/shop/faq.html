Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, payments, returns, rewards, and your account.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add a case to your bag, then continue to checkout. Choose M-Pesa before delivery or pay on delivery where available.

How long does delivery take?

Timing depends on your location. We update the order timeline as it moves from queue to shipping and delivery.

How does M-Pesa payment work?

Enter the M-Pesa number that should receive the prompt. Your order is confirmed after successful payment.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Select Pay on delivery at checkout and payment is collected when your order arrives.

Can I track my order?

Sign in, open Your orders, and choose Track delivery.

Can I cancel an order?

Eligible orders can be cancelled within three minutes while still on queue.

How do rewards points work?

Points from eligible purchases and reviews can be used as KSh credit at checkout.

How do I reset my password?

Select Forgot password on the login page and follow the email link.
